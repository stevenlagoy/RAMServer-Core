# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The RAMServer-Core project aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful and professional.
- Discuss technical disagreements on their merits.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than individuals.
- Keep project discussions relevant and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the RAMServer-Core project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Include relevant context and links where possible. Do not post private information publicly. Maintainers will review reports and determine an appropriate response.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, close discussions, or take other actions proportionate to the conduct. Decisions should be made consistently and with regard for the safety and well-being of participants.
